# FixYo Landing Page — Design Brainstorm

## Context
FixYo is a full-stack job management and quoting platform for Australian tradespeople. The audience is blue-collar professionals (plumbers, electricians, carpenters, etc.) who need practical, trustworthy tools. The design must feel rugged yet modern — not corporate, not startup-cute.

## Idea A — "Industrial Precision" (Brutalist Utility)
**Design Movement:** Industrial Brutalism meets Modern SaaS
**Core Principles:**
- Raw utility — no decoration for decoration's sake
- High contrast, bold typography as the primary visual element
- Grid-based but asymmetric — columns break intentionally
- Honest, direct language that respects the tradesperson's time

**Color Philosophy:** Deep charcoal (#1A1A1A) base with safety-orange (#FF6B2C) as the sole accent. Orange references hi-vis vests, warning tape, and construction energy. White text only. No gradients.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-bleed sections with hard edges. Oversized section numbers (01, 02, 03) as typographic anchors. Features presented in a dense grid with thick borders.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thick 3px horizontal rule dividers
- Oversized bold numerals as decorative anchors
- Feature cards with hard left-border accent lines

**Interaction Philosophy:** Instant, no-delay. Hover states are immediate color flips, not fades. Scroll triggers are sharp, not floaty.

**Animation:** Stagger-in on scroll with 0.1s delay per item. No parallax. No floating elements.

**Typography System:** `Barlow Condensed` (900 weight) for headings + `DM Sans` (400/500) for body. Uppercase headings with wide letter-spacing.

## Idea B — "Trusted Craft" (Warm Professional) ← SELECTED
**Design Movement:** Warm Modernism — think Notion meets a well-worn leather tool belt
**Core Principles:**
- Trustworthy and grounded — tradespeople are skeptical of flashy tech
- Warm off-white backgrounds with deep slate navy as the primary color
- Feature-dense but breathable — information is organized, not crammed
- Subtle depth through layering: cards float on backgrounds, sections have gentle texture

**Color Philosophy:** Background: warm cream `#FAFAF7`. Primary: deep navy `#1B2B4B`. Accent: amber-gold `#F59E0B`. Secondary text: slate `#64748B`. The warmth signals approachability; the navy signals reliability; the gold signals value/premium.

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric two-column hero (text left, app mockup right). Features in alternating left/right rows. Pricing in a 3-column card layout. Testimonials in a horizontal scroll strip.

**Signature Elements:**
- Subtle dot-grid texture on hero background
- Amber accent underlines on key headings
- Feature icons in rounded square containers with navy fill

**Interaction Philosophy:** Smooth and confident. Hover states use gentle lift (translateY -2px) + shadow deepening. CTAs pulse subtly on load.

**Animation:** Fade-up on scroll with 0.15s stagger. Hero elements animate in sequence: badge → headline → subtext → CTAs → mockup.

**Typography System:** `Sora` (700/800) for headings + `Inter` (400/500) for body. Clean hierarchy with generous line-height.

## Idea C — "Site Foreman" (Dark Command Center)
**Design Movement:** Dark Dashboard Aesthetic — like a site foreman's command screen
**Core Principles:**
- Dark mode first — slate-900 base with bright accent
- Data-forward: show the product UI prominently in the hero
- Neon-teal accent for interactive elements
- Dense information hierarchy that rewards careful reading

**Color Philosophy:** Background: `#0F172A` (slate-900). Surface: `#1E293B`. Accent: `#14B8A6` (teal). Text: `#F1F5F9`. The dark palette suggests professionalism and focus; teal references precision instruments and digital tools.

**Layout Paradigm:** Full-width dark hero with a floating app dashboard screenshot. Feature sections use dark cards on slightly lighter backgrounds. Stats bar across the full width.

**Signature Elements:**
- Glowing teal borders on feature cards
- Animated stat counters
- App UI screenshot with subtle glow/shadow

**Interaction Philosophy:** Fluid and techy. Hover states glow. Buttons have subtle shimmer animations.

**Animation:** Smooth entrance with blur-to-clear effect. Parallax on hero image. Counter animations on stats.

**Typography System:** `Space Grotesk` (700) for headings + `IBM Plex Sans` (400) for body. Monospace accents for numbers/codes.

## Selected Design: **Idea B — "Trusted Craft"**

Warm Modernism with deep navy, warm cream, and amber-gold. This approach resonates most with Australian tradespeople who value trust, reliability, and practical tools over flashy aesthetics. The warm palette differentiates FixYo from cold corporate SaaS competitors while maintaining professional credibility.

# FixYo — Stripe Business Description

## Primary Description (For Stripe Account Setup)

**FixYo** is a comprehensive business management platform designed specifically for Australian tradespeople and service professionals. We provide an all-in-one solution for managing jobs, creating professional quotes, generating invoices, tracking payments, and maintaining customer relationships.

### What We Offer:

**Job Management System** — Track and manage service jobs with real-time status updates, customer assignments, scheduling, and photo documentation of before-and-after work.

**Professional Quoting** — Create detailed quotes with line items, automatic calculations, tax rates, and discounts. Customers can review and electronically sign quotes directly through a secure portal.

**Invoice Generation** — Generate professional invoices with automatic line item calculations, tax handling, and payment tracking. Customers receive secure links to view invoices and make online payments.

**Customer Database** — Maintain comprehensive customer records with contact information, service history, quote history, and payment records all in one place.

**Online Payments** — Accept payments directly through the platform via Stripe, providing customers with a seamless checkout experience and automatic payment confirmation.

**Business Dashboard** — Real-time visibility into active jobs, pending quotes, outstanding invoices, and recent activity to help manage cash flow and business operations.

## Short Version (For Stripe Dashboard)

FixYo is a SaaS platform that helps Australian tradespeople manage jobs, quotes, invoices, and customer relationships. We enable service professionals to create professional quotes, track jobs with photos, generate invoices, and accept online payments from customers.

## Target Market

Australian tradespeople including plumbers, electricians, builders, painters, HVAC technicians, and other service professionals who need professional business management tools.

---

## Use Case Example

A plumbing business uses FixYo to:
1. Create a quote for a customer with line items (labor, materials, GST)
2. Send the quote to the customer via secure link
3. Customer reviews and electronically signs the quote
4. Quote is converted to a job with photo documentation
5. Upon completion, an invoice is generated
6. Customer pays online through the invoice link
7. Payment is automatically confirmed and recorded
